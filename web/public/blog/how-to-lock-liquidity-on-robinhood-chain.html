How to lock liquidity on Robinhood Chain.

Published 2026-07-24 · HoodLock Team

Locking liquidity is the single strongest trust signal a token project can send. On Robinhood Chain (chain id 4663), you can lock LP tokens or any ERC-20 in under a minute using HoodLock — a trustless locker with a verified contract, no admin keys, and a public proof page for every lock.

What you need

  • A wallet on Robinhood Chain (MetaMask, Rabby, Phantom, Robinhood Wallet or any WalletConnect-compatible wallet)
  • The token or LP contract address you want to lock
  • A small amount of ETH for gas plus the flat 0.005 ETH platform fee

Step-by-step: lock tokens in under a minute

  1. Open the app. Go to hoodlock.tech/app/locks and connect your wallet. The app adds Robinhood Chain automatically if your wallet doesn't have it yet.
  2. Pick the token. Paste the token or LP contract address — or simply pick it from the dropdown that lists every token in your wallet. The app verifies the symbol, decimals and your balance on-chain.
  3. Set the amount. Type it or hit MAX.
  4. Choose the unlock date. Use the presets (7D, 1M, 3M, 6M, 1Y) or pick a custom date. Remember: locks are extend-only — you can always push the date later, never earlier.
  5. Approve and lock. Two wallet confirmations: an ERC-20 approval, then the lock itself. Done.

Share the proof

Every lock gets a public proof page like hoodlock.tech/app?lock=0 that anyone can open — no wallet needed. It shows the amount, token, owner, unlock time and a direct link to the lock transaction on Blockscout. Drop it in your Telegram, pin it on X, put it in your docs. The contract speaks for itself.

Why HoodLock's locker can be trusted

  • Verified source code — the locker at 0xD0f7…C32F is verified on Blockscout; anyone can read every line.
  • No admin path to funds — nobody, including HoodLock, can touch locked tokens.
  • Owner-only withdrawal — only the lock owner can withdraw, and only after the unlock time.
  • Extend-only — unlock dates can be pushed later but never shortened.
  • Flat fee — 0.005 ETH per lock. No percentage cuts of your tokens, ever.
